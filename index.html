Where a small site can get stuck

A useful website can still have pages that are hard to find, read, or understand.

"I don't know which pages search tools can read."

We check page responses, links, sitemaps, metadata, and browser-rendered content. The report points to specific URLs.

"I know something is wrong, but not what to fix first."

We prioritize a few changes you can review and approve. The pilot keeps the work small enough to finish and verify.

"I need to know what the work achieved."

We record the starting state and show what changed on the site. Search ranking, AI mentions, and sales depend on systems outside our control.
