A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Before you start.

Is this SEO, or something different?

SEO helps search engines access and understand your pages. GEO considers how AI-assisted search discovers and represents that information. We start with useful content and a technically sound site. Read about GEO.

Will you guarantee rankings or AI mentions?

No. We agree on website work and verify the changes. Rankings, mentions, traffic and sales also depend on systems outside our control.

Can you work with my existing website?

Share the address and tell us how it is managed. Adam checks the platform and access before quoting work. An inquiry does not give us permission to change the site.

Will you automatically publish to my accounts?

The initial pilot uses an agreed release process. Social drafts in the demo are previews. A live account connection and its publishing permissions must be set up separately.
