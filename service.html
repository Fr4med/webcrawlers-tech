for your project.

Start with a conversation

The site invites you to discuss an assisted pilot. Submitting the form is not a purchase, a subscription or an instruction to change your website. Adam confirms fit, scope, access and price with you before paid work starts.

What the initial pilot covers

The starting offer is one live website, one language and up to 30 agreed pages. The repair sprint covers up to three agreed technical fixes and one existing-page clarity change, with a before-and-after check and a follow-up on those fixes after 30 days. Setup and delivery are quoted separately. Additional content work is scoped separately.

Access and release

You must control the website or have authority to approve work on it. The setup confirms appropriate access, backups and the release process. Changes are reviewed before the agreed publication step. Social account connections and permissions are separate from access to your website.

What we measure

We report the work performed, the checks completed and the remaining issues. Search rankings, AI recommendations, traffic, leads and revenue are not guaranteed. A sample demo is not a claim about customer results.

Questions or changes

Email hello@webcrawlers.tech to discuss a quote, scope change or an existing project. Payment, cancellation and delivery terms must be agreed for the specific project before work begins.
