Start with a conversation

The site invites you to discuss your business website, a visibility check and agreed repairs or additions. You can also enquire if someone else manages your site or you do not have one yet. Submitting the form is not a purchase, a subscription or an instruction to change your website. We agree on one website goal and a bounded set of pages. Adam reviews the findings with you, confirms the exact repairs, and obtains your approval before release. Scope, access and price are confirmed before paid work starts.

What the initial pilot covers

The starting offer is one live website, one language and up to 30 agreed pages. The repair sprint covers up to three agreed technical fixes and one existing-page clarity change, with a before-and-after check and a follow-up on those fixes after 30 days. Additional content or a new website needs its own agreed scope.

Access and release

We can check public pages without editing the site. Before changes begin, you must control the website or have authority to approve work on it. We confirm appropriate access, backups and the release process. Changes are reviewed before the agreed publication step. Social account connections and permissions are separate from access to your website.

What we measure

We report the work performed, the checks completed and the remaining issues. Search rankings, AI recommendations, traffic, leads and revenue are not guaranteed. Illustrative examples are not claims about customer results.

Questions or changes

Email hello@webcrawlers.tech to discuss a quote, scope change or an existing project. Payment, cancellation and delivery terms must be agreed for the specific project before work begins.
