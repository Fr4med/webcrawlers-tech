The starting scope

One live website, one language and up to 30 agreed pages. The website check and repair service includes up to three agreed technical fixes and one clarity change on an existing page. We check the affected pages after release and follow up on those fixes 30 days after the agreed release date.

The count is a limit, not a promise to fix every problem found. The written quote lists each included change and its acceptance check. A new website, extra content or ongoing monitoring has a separate scope.

What counts as a fix?

These examples help define a quote. They are not automatic inclusions or a price list.

A SMALL, DEFINED CHANGE

Correct a broken contact link

Replace an agreed link target in the accessible source, then check the affected page and destination. A broken form service or a failing email provider is a different job.

SCOPE DEPENDS ON THE PLATFORM

Correct a page address or title

Update a preferred page address or title in an editable page. A template used across many pages, plugin conflict or routing problem needs investigation and its own limits.

ONE EXISTING PAGE

Make the offer easier to understand

Revise agreed copy and the next action on one existing page using facts you confirm. New research, additional pages, a redesign or translation needs a separate agreement.

What can we do on your platform?

Public-page checks can cover WordPress, Shopify, Webflow, Wix, Squarespace, Framer and custom sites when the pages are accessible. We confirm coverage for your site before agreeing the audit. A public-page check does not grant editing access or prove that a particular repair is supported.

Websites with editable source files

Where the source and build are available, we can prepare specific HTML, content and metadata changes for review. We confirm repository permission, a preview, backups and the release process first.

Hosted website builders

Available edits depend on your plan, permissions, theme and plugins. We check the exact operation before quoting direct changes. If editing is restricted, the deliverable can be an agreed change sheet for your provider.

Platform-wide automatic repair is not part of this offer. If we cannot safely make a change with the available access, Adam explains the limitation and agrees a handover or a separate scope before work continues.

Access, review and release

You must own the website or have authority to approve the work. Public checks do not need your password. For repairs, we agree the minimum required access, backups, who releases the change and how it can be restored.

You review the proposed changes before the agreed publication step. Access to analytics, social accounts or payment systems is separate from website access. Do not send passwords or customer records in the enquiry form.

Revisions and work outside the quote

The quote states the revision allowance and how acceptance is checked. A request that changes the agreed outcome, adds pages or depends on a third party is reviewed before further work. Adam explains any change to price or dates and waits for your approval.

Large rebuilds, migrations, paid tools, advertising, third-party fees and open-ended monitoring are not included by a count of three fixes. Payment, cancellation and delivery terms are agreed for the specific project.

What we report

The report shows the work performed, the checks completed and what remains unresolved. Rankings, AI recommendations, traffic, enquiries and revenue are separate outcomes and are not guaranteed.

The initial enquiry and scope conversation are free. A deeper audit or repair starts only after its written scope and price are accepted. Read the quote route or see the delivery stages.
