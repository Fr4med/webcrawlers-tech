FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is WebCrawlers offering today?

A founder-led pilot for one small live website. We inspect up to 30 agreed pages in one language, review the findings with you, make up to three agreed technical fixes and one clarity change to an existing page, then verify the live result. Adam runs setup and delivery.

What is GEO?

Generative Engine Optimization means making a website easier for AI-assisted search tools to access, understand, and cite. The pilot can address site issues relevant to that goal, but no provider can guarantee a mention in an AI answer. Read the guide.

Does the software fix my site automatically?

No. The software gathers crawl, audit, and browser evidence. A person reviews it. We propose specific changes, agree on backups and release steps, and only publish after your approval.

How much does the pilot cost?

We quote the one-time setup with Adam and the repair sprint as separate line items after seeing the site. There is no published flat price or free trial for this pilot.

What do I receive?

A prioritized report, the agreed site changes, a before-and-after check of affected pages, and a 30-day follow-up on those fixes. We mark unavailable measurements as unavailable.

Will my rankings, AI mentions, or sales improve?

We cannot guarantee those outcomes. Search and AI answer systems make their own choices. We can show what the site returned before and after our work and identify remaining issues.

Do I need a website?

Yes. This pilot is for an owner-led business with a live site that we can inspect and, with permission, edit. We can discuss other needs separately.
