What is WebCrawlers offering today?

Website checks and agreed repairs, with Adam as your contact. The initial audit and repair scope covers one website, up to 30 agreed pages in one language, up to three agreed technical fixes and one page-clarity change. We check the affected live pages and follow up on those repairs 30 days after the agreed release date.

Can my agents use WebCrawlers?

We are building one WebCrawlers connection that brings the APIs behind our supported tools together. Through MCP, the Model Context Protocol, your agents will be able to use those tools and receive results without connecting to each provider separately. The customer platform is in development and is not yet publicly available. You can currently work directly with Adam on agreed website improvements.

What is GEO?

Generative Engine Optimization means making a website easier for AI-assisted search tools to access, understand, and cite. The website check and repair service can address site issues relevant to that goal, but no provider can guarantee a mention in an AI answer. Read the guide.

Does the software fix my site automatically?

The planned self-service product will let people and agents run audits and receive reports without waiting for a manual review. Making changes to a website is a separate permission. For direct implementation, we agree the changes, access and release steps with you first.

How is the price agreed?

The initial enquiry and scope conversation are free. A deeper audit or repair needs a written quote accepted before work starts. The quote identifies audit and repair work separately or as one package, with the price, currency, applicable tax treatment and payment milestones. See the quote route.

Is the first website check free?

The enquiry and initial scope conversation are free. They do not include a full audit. Any deeper website check or repair needs an accepted written quote. A planned free self-service check is not currently available through this public website.

When will Adam reply and deliver the work?

Adam replies within 2 business days. Delivery is quoted separately. Include your deadline in the enquiry; the written quote confirms delivery dates and access dependencies. The repair follow-up is 30 days after the agreed release.

Does my website platform support repairs?

We can check accessible public pages across common platforms. Direct changes depend on the exact platform, permissions, plan and issue. If your builder or provider limits editing, we can agree a change sheet for them. See platform and access limits.

How large is a fix, and how many revisions are included?

The quote lists each change and its revision allowance. Correcting one broken link is different from rebuilding a template or form service. Larger work is scoped and priced before it starts. Compare the fix examples.

What do I receive?

A prioritized report, the agreed site changes, a before-and-after check of affected pages, and a follow-up on those fixes 30 days after the agreed release. We mark unavailable measurements as unavailable.

What do you guarantee?

We guarantee delivery of the tools, connections or implementation agreed for your project. Our goal is to maximize your visibility in search and AI. We cannot guarantee that your business will appear in any AI chat or achieve a particular ranking.

What if I do not have a website?

Tell us what your business offers and how customers contact you today. We can discuss the website you need. A new website has its own agreed scope rather than the existing-site repair scope.

Can you help if someone else manages my website?

Yes. We can check the public pages and explain what needs attention. Repairs need permission from the site owner and access to make the approved changes. Tell Adam who manages the site when you enquire.
