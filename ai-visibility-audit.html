AI VISIBILITY AUDIT

How do I make my website
visible in AI search?

Start by checking whether search systems can access your pages and understand your business. Then give people useful, specific information they can trust. WebCrawlers helps small businesses do that work, with Adam managing the setup and agreed repairs.

What we check

  • Access. HTTP responses, robots.txt, page indexing directives, canonical URLs, sitemaps and internal links.
  • Indexing evidence. Available Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster records. A page returning HTTP 200 does not establish that it is indexed.
  • Your offer. Whether a visitor can tell what you do, who it is for, what they receive and how to contact you.
  • Useful evidence. Original examples, clearly sourced explanations, accurate business details and real results you have permission to publish.
  • AI answers. An agreed set of questions, recorded with the platform, date, language, search mode and cited sources. We distinguish a brand mention from a linked citation and an enquiry.

What you receive

A report linking each finding to its page and evidence, a prioritized repair plan, and verification of the changes we agree to make. The starting pilot covers one website, one language and up to 30 agreed pages. Setup and delivery are quoted separately before paid work. Read a sample finding or see how pricing works.

We ran this audit on our own site

On 29 September 2026, our crawler fetched 14 pages and found email links rewritten to a URL that returned 404 without JavaScript. We disabled that rewrite for our public site, corrected our service pages and crawled the published version again. The follow-up fetched all 15 public pages with no page errors or findings under our current technical checks. This is evidence of a specific repair. We have not established a resulting increase in AI citations, search traffic or enquiries.

Why a chatbot might not mention you

A broad question can have many reasonable answers. Missing from one answer does not identify the cause. We first check access and indexing, then compare the pages and businesses actually cited for relevant questions. An answer without web search may use older information. Repeated, recorded checks are more useful than a single screenshot.

What the platforms say

OpenAI documents OAI-SearchBot for search and treats its training crawler separately. Anthropic documents Claude-SearchBot for search. Google recommends useful original content and normal search fundamentals. None of these establishes a guaranteed place in an AI answer.

What we do not promise

We do not guarantee rankings, AI recommendations, traffic or sales. Adding a metadata tag or an AI text file does not prove improved visibility. The agreed website changes and their checks are the deliverable; mentions and enquiries are measured separately.
