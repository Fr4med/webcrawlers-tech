Pricing

Two items, quoted before work begins

We offer a supervised pilot for one live small-business website. We review the site and agree on scope before quoting a fee. The quote has two separate line items.

One-time setup with Adam

Plan for about 2–3 hours with Adam. We confirm the business facts, select up to 30 pages in one language, establish site access, and agree on backups and acceptance checks.

Repair sprint

We inspect the pages, deliver a prioritized report, make up to three agreed technical fixes and one existing-page clarity change, verify affected live pages, and follow up on those fixes after 30 days.

The price depends on the site and the changes agreed. The pilot has no automatic fixes, ongoing monitoring, subscription, or 30-day free trial. We do not guarantee rankings, AI mentions, traffic, or sales.
