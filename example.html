NORTHLINE STUDIO / SAMPLE

Make the services page understandable in search.

Example finding

What we found

The sample services page uses the title "Home". It does not identify the business or explain what the page offers.

<title>Home</title>

Proposed change

Use a distinct title that accurately describes the page and the business.

<title>Film editing services | Northline Studio</title>

How we'd check it

  • Confirm the service is an approved business fact.
  • Review the exact change and record the original.
  • Check the released page returns the new title.
  • Keep remaining issues and unverified outcomes visible.

A corrected title is a verified website change. It is not evidence of higher rankings or more inquiries.
