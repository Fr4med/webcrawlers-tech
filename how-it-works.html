HOW IT WORKS

From a website question
to checked changes.

Tell Adam what your business needs. We check what customers can find, agree useful changes and complete the work you approve.

Start enquiryScope & quotes

First, a free scope conversation

Your enquiry and initial discussion are free. Adam confirms the business goal, who manages the site and whether the work is a fit. A full audit or repair needs a written quote accepted before paid work starts.

  1. 01

    Check the agreed pages

    After the audit scope is agreed, we check the pages and save the findings.

    What happens at this stage?

    Checks cover the agreed public pages, links, titles, headings and page addresses. Adam also reviews how a visitor understands the business. Blocked pages, unavailable measurements and login restrictions are recorded as limits.

    You confirm the website and the business facts. Adam supplies a prioritized report and explains the useful next steps. See the report contents.

  2. 02

    Agree the changes and dates

    Choose the repairs that fit your goal and confirm the work in writing.

    What must be agreed?

    The scope names the changes, price, delivery date, revision allowance and who will implement and release them. Adam confirms access and backups before editing. If your provider manages the site, we agree their part in the work.

    New requirements can change the price or dates. We explain that change and obtain approval before extending the work.

  3. 03

    Make and check the agreed changes

    Review the prepared changes, approve the release and receive the results.

    What happens after implementation?

    Adam prepares the agreed changes for review. After the authorized release, the affected live pages are checked. You receive the before-and-after results, any remaining issues and the next action.

    The follow-up on agreed repairs is due 30 days after the agreed release date. Its scope is those repairs. Ongoing monitoring or new work requires a separate agreement.

When will it happen?

Your first reply

Adam replies within 2 business days. Include any deadline in your brief. The reply confirms the next step and any information needed for a quote. Delivery is quoted separately.

The work and follow-up

The quote confirms the audit and repair delivery dates. Required access, customer approvals and another provider's availability can affect the schedule. Any revised date is discussed with you.

Keep the enquiry reference. For a status request, email hello@webcrawlers.tech with that reference.

Adam is responsible for scope, review and delivery. This is an assisted website check and repair service. Automatic publishing, ongoing monitoring and a hosted customer dashboard are not included.
