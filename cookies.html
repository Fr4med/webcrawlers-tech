Updated 7 October 2026

This page describes storage on the public WebCrawlers website. Optional enquiry source context starts off disabled. You can send an enquiry with essential storage only.

Change your choice

You can also use Cookie settings in the footer of any page. Refusing or withdrawing optional context clears the saved source context in this tab. It does not remove enquiries you already sent.

What we store

StoragePurposeLifetime
webcrawlers_privacy
First-party preference cookie		Remembers your essential-only or optional-context choice, the policy revision and the time of your choice. It contains no name, email or visitor identifier.180 days from your last choice, or until you remove it.
webcrawlers-inquiry-entry-v1
Optional session storage		With your permission, keeps a limited entry-page path, an allowlisted referring host and short campaign labels. This context is sent only with an enquiry.Up to 30 minutes. Age is checked on page load and before submission. Expired context is ignored and cleared when checked. Refusal clears it immediately in this tab.

The preference cookie applies to this host and the site path /. It uses SameSite=Lax and Secure on HTTPS. We do not set a shared parent-domain cookie. Session storage follows the browser's tab-session lifecycle, including any tab restore, subject to the age check above.

Essential storage and enquiries

The enquiry form keeps a request reference in memory while it is open so an unchanged retry uses the same reference. A changed request with an unconfirmed receipt is held for review instead of automatically creating a second enquiry. Optional context is separate from this retry protection.

No advertising or analytics vendor added

This preference feature does not load third-party analytics scripts, advertising cookies or tracking pixels. Hosting providers may process request metadata needed for delivery and security. Those processes are separate from optional browser storage controlled here.

Email choices are separate

Allowing optional enquiry context does not subscribe you to a mailing list. The enquiry form asks separately for permission to process your details and contact you about that enquiry.

Read Privacy and your project information for how enquiry information is handled. Contact hello@webcrawlers.tech with questions or a request about an enquiry you already sent.
