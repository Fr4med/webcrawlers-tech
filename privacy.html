When you send an inquiry

We collect the contact details, business description, website address and project notes you choose to send. Adam Krestol uses this information to respond, assess the project and discuss its scope. Sending an inquiry does not subscribe you to a marketing list.

Cookies and optional enquiry context

Optional source context starts off disabled. The first-party webcrawlers_privacy cookie remembers your choice for 180 days. You can choose essential-only, allow optional context, or change your choice through Cookie settings. Sending an enquiry does not depend on allowing optional context, and cookie choices are separate from email permission.

With your permission, this browser tab keeps a sanitized entry-page path, an allowlisted referring host and short campaign source, medium and campaign labels in session storage. Other query details and full referring URLs are discarded. We do not store browsing transcripts. Context is reused across navigation for up to 30 minutes. Its age is checked on page load and before submission; expired, malformed or missing context is treated as unknown. Withdrawing permission clears this tab's saved context and prevents an already-open form from sending it.

When you submit, permitted source context and the current form-page path can accompany your enquiry. Essential-only submissions include no referring host or campaign labels. Your request reference protects unchanged retries. If a previous receipt is unconfirmed and the payload changes, including after you withdraw permission, the form preserves the original reference and asks you to contact Adam instead of sending a possible duplicate. Read Cookies and browser storage for names, purposes and lifetimes.

Where it goes

The public website is hosted on GitHub Pages. The inquiry form sends information to WebCrawlers' Cloudflare endpoint. Inquiry records are stored for follow-up and can be synchronized to Adam's project-management system. Infrastructure providers may process request metadata for delivery and security.

For enquiry review, your name, email address and message may also be sent through Cloudflare Email Routing or Cloudflare Email Service to the owner's Gmail inbox. This is an owner notification, not a marketing message or an automatic confirmation email to you. Email delivery does not guarantee that a person has read your enquiry.

Keep sensitive information out of the form

Do not send passwords, API keys, payment details or private customer records. If we work together, we agree on the necessary access and data handling separately. We do not treat an inquiry as permission to access or change your accounts.

Your choices

Contact hello@webcrawlers.tech to ask what inquiry information we hold, correct it or request deletion. We may need to verify that the request is yours. There is currently no automatic retention schedule; deletion requests are handled by Adam.

Contact

WebCrawlers is operated by Adam Krestol. Email hello@webcrawlers.tech with questions about your information.
