USING THIS WEBSITE

Accessibility

WebCrawlers is operated by Adam Krestol. We want people with disabilities to be able to read our information and contact us without unnecessary barriers.

Updated 21 October 2026. This statement covers the public website at webcrawlers.tech.

Display and navigation

  • Use the Accessibility button on each page to enlarge text up to 200%, increase contrast, underline links or reduce motion. These preferences stay on this device and do not send analytics.
  • Use Tab and Shift+Tab to move between controls, Enter or Space to activate them, and Escape to close dialogs and the mobile menu.
  • A Skip to content link moves to the main page. Controls have labels and visible keyboard focus.
  • Browser zoom is enabled. The site adapts to narrow screens and respects your device's reduced-motion preference.
  • Form errors identify fields that need attention. You can correct an enquiry before sending it. An unconfirmed receipt keeps the same reference for retry.
  • Optional analytics are off until you choose them. They are not needed to send an enquiry.

Need another way to contact us?

Email hello@webcrawlers.tech. Tell Adam which page or action was difficult and the format or assistance you need. Include your browser or assistive technology if useful. Do not send passwords, payment information or medical details.

You can request service information and send a project enquiry by email if the form is difficult to use. We will work with you on a suitable alternative.

Our approach and remaining limitations

We use the Israeli website-accessibility guidance and WCAG 2.2 Level AA criteria as our development and review targets. Display controls support this work; they do not replace accessible content and navigation.

Automated checks and keyboard/mobile reviews help us find problems. A complete independent assessment with assistive technologies has not yet been completed, so we do not claim certification. Downloaded documents and third-party websites may have different accessibility. Ask for an alternative format if you need one.

This is an online service. Contact Adam by email to discuss any accessibility arrangements needed for a specific engagement.

מידע נגישות בעברית

האתר מופעל על ידי אדם קרסטול. כפתור הנגישות מאפשר הגדלת טקסט, ניגודיות גבוהה, סימון קישורים והפחתת תנועה. אפשר לנווט באמצעות המקלדת ולהשתמש בזום הדפדפן.

לקבלת עזרה, דיווח על קושי או בקשת מידע בפורמט חלופי, פנו אל hello@webcrawlers.tech. ציינו את העמוד והפעולה שבה נתקלתם בקושי. אין צורך לשלוח מידע רפואי. טרם הושלמה בדיקת נגישות עצמאית מלאה.
