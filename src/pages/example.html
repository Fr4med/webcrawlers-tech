One broken link, 14 findings

14 broken-link findings → 0

Both checks covered 14 pages. All the original broken-link findings pointed to one email-protection address.

What was wrong and how we checked

Our website check on 29 September 2026 fetched 14 pages and found one failed email-protection address. That address returned HTTP 404, meaning the destination was not found. Fourteen broken-link findings pointed to it.

  1. We saved the original bounded HTTP crawl and the failing destination.
  2. We corrected the email link rewriting and recorded the scoped email-obfuscation repair in the dated operator review.
  3. We repeated the 14-page crawl. It recorded no page errors and no findings under the selected checks.

After adding another page, we saved an expanded 15-page check separately.

These are technical crawl observations. They do not establish improved rankings, AI citations, traffic or enquiries. The immediate before and after run records do not retain an exact published source/configuration revision.
