Think of a shopfront repair. First check what is wrong, agree the work and price, then check the finished repair. Your website follows the same steps.

01 / CHECK

Check your website visibility

Can customers find your online shop or business website? Is it clear what you sell and how to contact you?

  • Agree which pages and business goal to check
  • Check access, links and the clarity of your offer
  • Explain the findings and what needs attention

02 / AGREE

Agree the repairs and quote

Choose the useful changes and know what they will cost before the work begins.

  • Choose repairs, content changes or additions
  • Confirm permission, access and backups
  • Agree the scope, written quote and release plan

03 / MAKE AND CHECK

Make the changes and check them live

We complete approved repairs or additions and check the affected pages after the agreed release.

  • Review the changes before publication
  • Check the live pages and record remaining issues
  • Follow up on the agreed repairs after 30 days

The initial website audit and repair scope covers one website, one language and up to 30 agreed pages, with up to three technical fixes and one page-clarity change. Extra content or a new website needs its own agreed scope. If someone else manages your site, tell us before we plan the changes.

There are no automatic fixes or ongoing monitoring in this offer. Rankings, AI mentions, traffic and sales are not guaranteed.
