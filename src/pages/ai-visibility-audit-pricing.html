What changes the quote?

The website and the issue

The number of agreed pages, the languages involved and whether the issue affects one page or a shared template. We check the platform and whether a repair is possible with the available access.

The work and the handover

New writing, larger code changes, work by your existing provider, revision requests and a tight deadline can change the scope. Third-party charges need separate agreement.

How to get a useful quote

Send your business name, website if you have one, the problem you want to solve and who manages the site. Add your deadline and any access limits. Adam confirms any missing information before preparing the quote.

Adam replies within 2 business days. Delivery is quoted separately after the scope and required access are clear. If your need is urgent, include the date you need help.
