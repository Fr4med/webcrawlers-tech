HOW IT WORKS

From a website question
to checked changes.

Tell Adam what your business needs. We check what customers can find, agree useful changes and complete the work you approve.

Start enquiryView evidence
  1. 01

    Check your website visibility

    Can customers find your website, understand your offer and contact you?

    How we check

    We agree the business goal and pages to check. Our tools request the agreed pages, follow allowed links and record page responses, titles, headings, preferred addresses and links. We also read the pages as a visitor would. If a page blocks access or needs a login, the report explains that limit.

    You do not need to manage the website yourself for us to check public pages. If you do not have a site, tell us what you offer and how customers reach you today.

  2. 02

    Agree the repairs and quote

    Review the findings and choose the changes that fit your business.

    What we agree

    Adam explains the findings in plain English and separates measured problems from suggestions and missing information. Research can add context about relevant questions and public sources. It does not establish that an AI assistant will recommend the business.

    We confirm permission, the access needed for repairs, backups and the release plan. You receive a written scope and quote before paid work. Changes only reach the live site after your approval.

  3. 03

    Make approved changes and check them live

    Complete the agreed work, check the affected pages and explain what remains.

    What you receive

    The initial website audit and repair scope covers one website, one language and up to 30 agreed pages. It includes up to three agreed technical fixes and one clarity change on an existing page. Additional content or a new website needs its own agreed scope.

    After the agreed release, we check the affected live pages and send a before-and-after report with the changes and remaining issues. A follow-up on those agreed repairs is included after 30 days.

Who handles the work?

Adam reviews the findings and handles delivery. This offer is an assisted service. Automatic publishing, ongoing monitoring and a hosted customer dashboard are not included.

We confirm scope and price before any paid check or repair. Rankings, AI mentions, traffic and sales are not guaranteed.
