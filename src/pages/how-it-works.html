THE PROCESS

Your website.
Five clear steps.

Adam handles one website, one language and up to 30 agreed pages.

Start enquiryView evidence
  1. 01

    Scope and setup

    Confirm the goal, access, scope and quote.

    Details

    We ask what the business offers, who the pages are for, and which URLs matter. We confirm that you control the site and can grant the access needed for approved repairs. We agree on a backup and release plan before changing anything. Setup has its own one-time fee in the quote.

  2. 02

    Crawl and inspect

    Inspect the agreed pages and record evidence.

    Details

    The crawler requests pages on your site, follows allowed links, and records responses, titles, headings, canonical URLs, and links. A browser-based page reader captures rendered pages where that adds useful evidence. Some pages can block access or require a login; the report notes those limits.

  3. 03

    Review the findings

    Review findings and the proposed repair plan.

    Details

    We check the output and prioritize specific problems. Research may add context about relevant questions and public sources, but we do not claim that an AI assistant will recommend your business. The report separates measured findings from suggestions and missing data.

  4. 04

    Agree on repairs

    Approve specific changes and the release plan.

    Details

    The repair sprint includes up to three agreed technical fixes and one clarity change on an existing page. The exact changes depend on the site. You review the plan and authorize release; changes do not ship automatically.

  5. 05

    Verify and follow up

    Check affected live pages and follow up after 30 days.

    Details

    We inspect affected live pages after release and send a before-and-after report with the changes and remaining issues. A follow-up on those agreed fixes is included after 30 days. It is not a daily or weekly monitoring subscription.

Current service availability

The crawler, audits, browser capture, and customer job records work as an operator-run system. Project inquiries are available on this site. Live publishing and a hosted customer dashboard are not enabled in this offer. The pilot relies on a human operator.

We quote setup and delivery separately before paid work begins. Search rankings, AI mentions, traffic, and sales cannot be guaranteed.
