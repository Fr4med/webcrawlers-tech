A clear, accessible website gives search tools useful information about what you offer. It cannot guarantee a place in an AI answer.

Generative Engine Optimization, often shortened to GEO, means making your website information easier for AI search tools to find, understand and cite. When a customer asks ChatGPT, Copilot, Gemini or Perplexity a question your business can answer, those tools may use information from the web. You can improve the information available about your business, but you cannot choose which answer a provider gives.

The term comes from the 2023 paper GEO: Generative Engine Optimization. Its researchers tested changes such as citing sources, adding statistics and quoting experts. The study measured visibility in its test setting. It does not establish that the same change will bring your business more enquiries or sales.

What customers may see

A search result can link to your website. An AI answer may summarize several sources and mention or link to your business. The answer can vary with the question, available search tools and the information those tools find.

If your website gives an unclear offer, an old address or a broken contact link, people and search tools may find information that does not help the customer. Those are useful problems to check first.

Search results and AI answers

Search Engine Optimization, or SEO, concerns how your website appears in search results. GEO concerns how your information may appear in an AI answer. Both depend on information that people and search tools can access and understand.

What to check on your website

  • Can customers reach the pages? Check broken links, pages that fail to load and the contact route.
  • Is the offer clear? Explain what you sell, who it is for and how to contact you.
  • Are the business details accurate? Keep the name, location, services and contact information consistent.
  • Can a reader check the claims? Use real examples, named sources and dates. Only publish results you have permission to use.
  • Is old information still useful? Correct details that are stale or contradictory.

We also check the site instructions and page addresses search tools use. If someone else manages your website, the report can explain which changes to discuss with them.

What this work can promise

You can agree specific website changes and check that they work on the live pages. AI answers are controlled by third parties and may change, even for the same question. Rankings, AI mentions, traffic and sales are not guaranteed.
