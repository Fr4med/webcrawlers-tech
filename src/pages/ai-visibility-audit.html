What we check
  • Access. HTTP responses, robots.txt, page indexing directives, canonical URLs, sitemaps and internal links.
  • Indexing evidence. Available Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster records for the agreed property, with the appropriate access. A page returning HTTP 200 does not establish that it is indexed.
  • Your offer. Whether a visitor can tell what you do, who it is for, what they receive and how to contact you.
  • Useful evidence. Original examples, clearly sourced explanations, accurate business details and real results you have permission to publish.
  • AI answers. Where separately agreed and available, a bounded set of questions recorded with the platform, date, language, search mode and cited sources. We distinguish a brand mention from a linked citation and an enquiry.
Our own-site audit

Our own website, checked on 29 September 2026. The crawl fetched 14 pages and recorded one failed discovered email-protection URL. Fourteen broken-link findings pointed to that same HTTP 404 destination. After the scoped email-rewrite correction, the repeat 14-page crawl recorded no page errors and no findings under the selected checks. An expanded 15-page crawl was saved separately after another page was added.

These are technical crawl observations. They do not establish improved rankings, AI citations, traffic or enquiries. The immediate before and after run records do not retain an exact published source/configuration revision.

Why a chatbot might not mention you

A broad question can have many reasonable answers. Missing from one answer does not identify the cause. We first check access and indexing, then compare the pages and businesses actually cited for relevant questions. An answer without web search may use older information. Repeated, recorded checks are more useful than a single screenshot.

Platform guidance

OpenAI documents OAI-SearchBot for search and treats its training crawler separately. Anthropic documents Claude-SearchBot for search. Google recommends useful original content and normal search fundamentals. None of these establishes a guaranteed place in an AI answer.

Measurement limits

We do not guarantee rankings, AI recommendations, traffic or sales. Adding a metadata tag or an AI text file does not prove improved visibility. The agreed website changes and their checks are the deliverable; mentions and enquiries are measured separately.
