Where customers may get stuck
  • Can people reach your pages? We check broken links, page responses and the instructions search tools use to access your site. This includes robots.txt, indexing directives, preferred page addresses, sitemaps and internal links.
  • Does search have your pages? With the appropriate permission, we review available Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster records for the agreed website. A page loading successfully with HTTP 200 does not prove it is indexed.
  • Is your offer clear? Can a visitor tell what you do, who it is for, what they receive and how to contact you?
  • Are your business details trustworthy? We look for accurate details, original examples, clearly sourced explanations and real results you have permission to publish.
  • What do AI answers say? Where agreed and available, we record a limited set of questions with the platform, date, language, search mode and cited sources. A brand mention, a linked citation and an enquiry are different observations.
A broken link we found on our own site

On 29 September 2026, our website check fetched 14 pages and found one failed email-protection address. Fourteen broken-link findings pointed to that same HTTP 404 destination. After the scoped email-rewrite correction, the repeat 14-page crawl recorded no page errors and no findings under the selected checks. We saved an expanded 15-page check separately after adding another page.

These are technical crawl observations. They do not establish improved rankings, AI citations, traffic or enquiries. The immediate before and after run records do not retain an exact published source/configuration revision.

Why might an AI answer leave out your business?

A broad question can have many reasonable answers. One missing mention does not tell us the cause. We first check access and indexing, then compare the pages and businesses actually cited for relevant questions. An answer without web search may use older information. Repeated, recorded checks tell us more than a single screenshot.

What the search providers say

OpenAI documents OAI-SearchBot for search and treats its training crawler separately. Anthropic documents Claude-SearchBot for search. Google recommends useful original content and normal search fundamentals. These are useful checks for a website, but they do not guarantee a place in an AI answer.

What this check can tell you

The deliverable is the agreed website work and the checks on those changes. Rankings, AI recommendations, traffic and sales are not guaranteed. Adding a metadata tag or an AI text file does not prove improved visibility. We measure mentions and enquiries separately.
