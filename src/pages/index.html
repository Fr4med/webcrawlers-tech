What do you guarantee?

We guarantee delivery of the tools, connections or implementation agreed for your project. Our goal is to maximize your visibility in search and AI. We cannot guarantee that your business will appear in any AI chat or achieve a particular ranking.

Can my agents use WebCrawlers?

We are building one WebCrawlers connection that brings the APIs behind our supported tools together. Through MCP, the Model Context Protocol, your agents will be able to use those tools and receive results without connecting to each provider separately. The customer platform is in development and is not yet publicly available. You can currently work directly with Adam on agreed website improvements.

Can you work with my existing site?

Share the address and tell us who manages it. We can check public pages. Changes need the site owner's permission and the right access.

What happens after I enquire?

Adam replies within two business days to discuss scope and price. Delivery timing is confirmed in the quote. Your enquiry does not authorize work, payment or publication.
